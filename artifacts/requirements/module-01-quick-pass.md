# Quick-Pass Starter Requirements (Module 01)

## 1. Five Explicit Requirements
1. Users can browse and search providers by category.
2. Users can view provider profiles containing ratings and reviews.
3. Users can book available time slots and pay through the platform.
4. Providers can configure their own availability, hourly pricing, and service descriptions.
5. Platform charges a 15% commission on provider earnings and generates confirmation emails.

## 2. Two Core Ambiguities
1. **Cancellation & Refund Mechanics**: How are provider-specific cancellation policies structured and enforced (e.g. cutoff windows vs tiered percentage refunds)?
2. **Search Performance Metric**: What exact latency threshold (e.g., p95 < 150ms) defines "search should feel instant" under target concurrent load?

## 3. Question for PM
- "Does the 15% platform commission apply universally across all service categories and tiers, or are there custom commission schedules for enterprise/vetted providers?"
